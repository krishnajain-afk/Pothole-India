    how to get nominated

    snap. pin. fixed.

    1. 01

      snap it. safely.

      stand on the footpath or somewhere safe. get the pothole and a bit of the road in the frame. daylight helps.

    2. 02

      we pin it.

      it goes on the wall with the city it’s from, and your handle if you want one.

    3. 03

      watch it get fixed.

      when one gets repaired, its tile gets a FIXED stamp. that’s the whole point.

    read this first

    no heroics. footpath only.

    • stay on the footpath or another safe spot. never stand or stop on the road.
    • never take the photo while you’re driving or riding. a friend or passenger does it.
    • daylight, no rush hour, and nobody (especially kids) near moving traffic.
    • keep other people’s faces and number plates out of the shot.
