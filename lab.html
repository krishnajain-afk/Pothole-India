dodge the ***hole.

your camera. your head. three lanes of potholes coming straight at you. lean to dodge. literally.

  1. allow the camera. sit or stand still and face the screen.
  2. lean left or right to switch lanes.
  3. hit one and we’ll tell you what a pothole really costs.

your camera feed stays on your device. nothing is recorded, saved or uploaded. head tracking runs in your browser (MediaPipe, by Google; the first load downloads about 4 MB). no camera? you can play with taps or the ← → keys.
